# INTERNAL MEMO - Frio Beverage Company
**To:** Executive Leadership Team
**From:** Corporate Strategy (M. Delgado)
**Date:** June 12, 2025
**Re:** Functional beverage category - enter now or hold?
**Classification:** Confidential - internal distribution only

## Summary
Strategy has completed a first-pass review of the functional beverage
category (energy, nootropic, prebiotic, hydration+). The category is
growing faster than every segment of our core portfolio. We are
recommending we HOLD and revisit in 12 months.

## What we know
- Category growth is strong and sustained; our core CSD business is
  approximately flat.
- Our Volt Focus pilot (TX c-stores, launched Q1) is early but trending
  well above plan on velocity and margin.
- Competitor majors are active around the category; valuations for
  independent functional brands are elevated.

## Open questions that drove the HOLD
1. **Cannibalization.** Would a functional launch under Volt branding pull
   share from Volt Sports rather than grow total portfolio revenue?
2. **Build vs acquire.** Elevated target valuations make acquisition
   expensive; but building risks 18-24 months of shelf-space fights our
   national distribution may or may not shortcut.
3. **Regulatory.** Novel functional ingredients (nootropics, adaptogens)
   carry unsettled FDA posture; legal wants a defined ingredient strategy
   before national commitment.

## Decision
Hold. Continue the Volt Focus pilot, expand tracking of category M&A, and
revisit with fresh market data by mid-2026.
